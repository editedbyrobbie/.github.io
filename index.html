MY NAME IS ROBBIE

And Here's Why I Do This

I believe every video is a story worth telling, whether it's a creator building something from nothing, an athlete fighting to prove people wrong, or someone trying to be better than they were yesterday.

There is always a story behind the footage, and that's what I aim to capture. Anyone can make a video look good:  but I want the viewer to feel something, to get pulled in as if they were there.

Because a story like that deserves more than a "good edit." It deserves to be felt, remembered, and told properly.

FAQ

Questions You Might Have

What kind of videos do you edit?

For the most part: long-form cinematic video essays. Boxing is my strongest area. plus creator vlogs, reaction cuts and highlight edits.

How much does a video cost?

It depends on length, footage volume and turnaround. Most creators land on a monthly retainer of four long-form videos, which works out cheaper per video than one-offs. Message me with your channel and I'll quote the same day.

How long does it take?

Seven days from the moment footage lands to final export, revisions included. If you need faster and the slot is free, I'll say so honestly rather than promise it and miss.

Do you help shape the story, not just the edit?

Yes — structuring the narrative is part of the job, especially for essay-style videos.

How do I get started?

Send me your channel and a link to recent raw footage on WhatsApp. If it's a fit, you get a quote and a start date within a day. If it isn't, I'll tell you who to speak to instead.

Why you over a cheaper editor?

A cheap editor cuts what you shot. I build the story around it: cold open, tension, payoff, sound design. The difference shows up in average view duration, not in the export settings.
